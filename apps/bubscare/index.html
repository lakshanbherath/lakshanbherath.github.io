Bubscare

A private, offline child health organizer for parents and caregivers.

   View on the App Store ↗

Care, kept on your device.

Bubscare helps parents and caregivers organize vaccinations, growth measurements, doctor visits and care contacts — with child health data stored only on your device.

Key features

Child profiles & photos
Vaccination schedules & records
Growth tracking & charts
Doctor visits & care contacts
Local reminders
Export & backup when you choose
GOOD TO KNOW

Bubscare is an organizational and reference tool only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional pediatric care.
